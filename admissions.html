Admissions

Begin your academic journey with a flexible and welcoming admissions process designed around your goals.

How to apply

  • Submit your online application form.
  • Upload academic transcripts and supporting documents.
  • Complete the personal statement or essay.
  • Attend an interview or counselling session if required.
  • Receive your offer and complete enrollment steps.

Important dates

  • Applications open: January 15
  • Early priority deadline: March 30
  • Final deadline: June 30
  • Classes begin: September 1

Admission requirements

Academic Record

High school transcripts or equivalent academic qualification.

English Proficiency

Proof of language readiness for international and non-native applicants.

Supporting Documents

ID, reference letters, and any relevant certificates when applicable.
