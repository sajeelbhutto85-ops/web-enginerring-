About North Valley

Our mission is to create an inspiring educational experience rooted in excellence, inclusion, and opportunity.

Our story

Founded with a vision to make quality education accessible and relevant, North Valley College has grown into a trusted learning community where students are empowered to discover their strengths and pursue meaningful careers.

We combine a strong academic foundation with collaborative learning, mentorship, and real-world application to prepare students for professional and personal success in a changing world.

  • Student-centered learning environment.
  • Expert teachers and career advisors.
  • Focus on innovation, ethics, and leadership.

At a glance

  • Established: 2004
  • Campus: Urban and green campus
  • Student body: 12,000+
  • Programs: 25+ fields

Our values

  • Academic integrity and excellence.
  • Inclusivity and belonging for every student.
  • Innovation through practice and inquiry.
  • Service, leadership, and community impact.
