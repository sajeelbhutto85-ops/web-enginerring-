Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about studying, admissions, and student life at North Valley College.

How do I apply?

Complete the online application and submit your academic records and supporting documents.

Are scholarships available?

Yes. We offer academic and need-based scholarship opportunities for eligible students.

Do you offer support services?

Yes. Students have access to advising, career support, wellness services, and learning assistance.

Can I visit campus?

Absolutely. Campus tours can be scheduled through the admissions office.

What programs are offered?

We offer programs in business, technology, health sciences, arts, and social sciences.

Is the campus inclusive?

Yes. We foster an inclusive learning environment with support for all students.
